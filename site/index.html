Fragrance Notes Add to Chrome

Know what a perfume smells like before you buy it

Fragrance Notes opens a small panel on the product page of more than 300 perfume shops, with the notes, accords, longevity and similar scents from Fragrantica.

Add to Chrome, it's free View the source

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and other Chromium browsers.

Everything you'd look up, without leaving the shop

Open a perfume in a shop you know and the panel finds it on Fragrantica for you. Close it, move it to another corner, or open it with a keyboard shortcut.

Notes
Top, middle and base notes. Click one to see what it smells like.
Main accords
The scent families that define it, as bars or as one colour strip.
When to wear
Which seasons and whether day or night, from the community's votes.
What people say
The pros and cons reviewers mention most.
Longevity and sillage
How long it lasts on skin and how far it projects.
Similar perfumes and dupes
Up to three of each, with pictures, so you can compare or find a cheaper alternative.
Price comparison optional
Turn on anonymous price sharing to see how each size compares with other shops in your country, where it’s cheapest, and the lowest price of the last 30 days.

Works where you already shop

Douglas, Sephora, Notino, The Perfume Shop, Ulta, Harrods, Saks, Galeries Lafayette, Jovoy, Luckyscent, Aedes and more than 300 other shops in Europe, the UK, the US and Australia.

Missing a shop? Add it yourself in the settings, or ask for it on GitHub.

No account. No tracking.

The extension looks perfumes up on Fragrantica straight from your browser. Nothing reaches us unless you turn on anonymous price sharing, and then only the perfume, size, price and shop.
